Where Totogi is stronger

  • A funded delivery organization: tier-1 integration programs, global support coverage, enterprise SLAs.
  • Managed-SaaS operations: they carry the pager; with self-hosted genalpha, you (or our hosting) do.
  • Market presence and analyst mindshare.

Where genalpha is stronger

  • Sovereignty and lock-in: open code on your infrastructure vs a proprietary generator on one US cloud.
  • Proof culture: a published, reproducible verification record instead of demos.
  • Total scope for small operators: growth stack and all channels included — no second SaaS stack, no frontend project.
  • Economics at small scale: viable from a few thousand lines.
