genalpha-bss vs ISP billing platforms (Splynx, Sonar)

Splynx and Sonar are mature, widely used ISP billing/management platforms — for a classic vertically integrated ISP they are strong, proven choices. genalpha-bss aims at a different shape of operator: one that sells over (or opens) shared/open-access networks, wants standards-based integration, and needs the commercial stack (marketing, channels, wholesale settlement) in the same product.

Honest guidance

If you are a single-network ISP whose top need is deep ACS/TR-069 device management today, Splynx or Sonar may serve you better right now. If you sell (or plan to sell) over open-access networks, want your growth engine and channels included, need Nordic invoicing/payment rails, or want software you can inspect and host anywhere — that is exactly what genalpha-bss is for.
